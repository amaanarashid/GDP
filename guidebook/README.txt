The one-paragraph pitch:
  Machines break down. This app watches their sensors, learns
  what "normal" looks like, and warns BEFORE a breakdown — so
  maintenance happens on your schedule, not the machine's. It
  has simulated machines for demonstration, and machine learning
  validated on REAL industrial data: trained only on healthy
  data from a metro air compressor, our detector caught a real
  oil-leak failure it had never seen, 51 hours before the
  repair, with a 1.3% false-alarm rate.
