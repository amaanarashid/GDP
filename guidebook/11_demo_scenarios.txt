11 — DEMO SCENARIOS: PROVING THE MODEL LEARNED INTERACTIONS
Predict-maintenance panel, ML Model page, AI4I 2020 dataset
==============================================================

All figures below were measured against the live model. If you
retrain, they should reproduce within a percent or two.

Baseline (dataset averages):
  Air temperature 300 K · Process temperature 310 K
  Rotational speed 1539 rpm · Torque 40 Nm · Tool wear 108 min


THE SCENARIO TABLE
------------------
 #  SCENARIO                SET THE SLIDERS TO           RISK    TIER
 1  Healthy baseline        all at average               0.0%    healthy
 2  Low speed alone         speed 1200                   1.5%    healthy
 3  Tool worn out           wear 240                    21.0%    watch

THE TWO COMPARISONS THAT WIN THE ARGUMENT
-----------------------------------------
★ ROWS 8 vs 9 — the surprise
  Everything at maximum scores 42.5%. The overstrain case, where
  SPEED IS LOWER, scores 87.4%.
  SAY: "Every individual reading is higher in the first case, yet
  the model is twice as worried about the second. It can't be
  reading thresholds — it learned that high torque AGAINST low
  speed is overstrain, while high torque WITH high speed is just
  a machine working hard."

★ ROWS 2 + 3 vs 9 — the whole is more than the sum
  Low speed alone 1.5%. Worn tool alone 21%. Both together with
  high torque: 87.4%.
  SAY: "1.5% plus 21% doesn't make 87%. The risk lives in the
  combination — exactly what a rules-based system cannot capture."


WHY THESE SCENARIOS EXIST (the physics in AI4I)
-----------------------------------------------
The dataset documents five failure modes, and four of them are
COMBINATIONS — which is the whole reason a model is needed:

  Tool wear failure     tool used 200-240 minutes
  Heat dissipation      air/process gap < 8.6 K AND speed < 1380
  Power failure         torque x speed outside 3.5-9 kW
  Overstrain            tool wear x torque above a threshold
  Random                0.1% baseline chance

Power reference: P = torque x speed x 2*pi/60
  healthy baseline   6.4 kW   (inside the safe band)
  power too low      1.6 kW   (below)
  power too high    17.7 kW   (above)
  everything maxed  23.2 kW   (far above, but coherent)


THE 30-SECOND DEMO
------------------
  1. Click "Healthy machine" -> Predict -> 0%, no action needed
  2. Click "Worn / overloaded" -> Predict -> 87%, service immediately
  3. Point at the sliders: "and notice speed went DOWN, not up.
     High torque against low speed is overstrain. The model learned
     that interaction from 10,000 examples; a threshold on any
     single sensor would have missed it entirely."

  If you have another 15 seconds, do scenario 8 as the punchline:
  drag everything to maximum, predict, and show it's only 42.5%.


IF ASKED "COULD YOU JUST USE IF-STATEMENTS?"
--------------------------------------------
"You could encode these four rules if you already knew them — but
they came from the dataset's documentation. On a real machine
nobody hands you the failure physics; that's what the model
recovers from data. And the feature-space plot shows it: failures
occupy a region, not a corner."
