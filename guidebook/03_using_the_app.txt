==============================================================
03 — USING THE APP: EVERY PAGE, IN EASY WORDS
==============================================================

DASHBOARD
  Your home screen. Every machine as a card with a rotating 3D
  model, health %, and status. Recent alerts on the right —
  click one to jump to that machine. Click any card to open the
  machine.

ML MODEL  (the star of the show)
  Opens with a big drop zone. Drag any labelled CSV onto it
  (datasets/ai4i2020.csv is perfect). The app reads the file,
  finds the sensors and failure column BY ITSELF, trains a
  model live, and shows:
    - animated score tiles (precision, recall, F1, AUC)
    - "what drives failure" importance bars
    - a risk timeline over held-out test rows — red dots are
      real failures; a good model puts them on the peaks
    - a ROC curve with a plain-English explainer
  All of that is about YOUR file only. Below the lab there's a
  separate card: "MetroPT-3 benchmark — click to view" — the
  models proven on real metro-compressor data. It's behind a
  click on purpose, so it's never mistaken for your upload.

MACHINE DETAIL  (click any machine)
  - 3D digital twin: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom. Parts are
    painted by live health (green/yellow/red), failing parts
    pulse red, fans spin and the press ram moves while running.
  - Maintenance priority: which part to service first, with
    "days left" per part. For simulated machines this is trend
    extrapolation, not ML (see 04) — it shrinks the moment a
    fault develops.
  - Live sensors, colored by severity.
  - ML analysis: "Train on history" then "Analyze with ML" —
    the machine's own anomaly detector (see 04).
  - Prediction accuracy: the app grading its own homework —
    stored predictions vs what actually happened.
  - Complete maintenance: THE button. Restores health to 100%
    and writes a record. Health never heals on its own — that's
    deliberate; it's a maintenance app.

SIMULATOR  (admin only)
  - Play/Pause: streams readings every 2 seconds; parts wear
    down over time like real machines.
  - Fault panel: inject a fault, watch sensors climb, alerts
    fire, health drop. Clear it or fix with maintenance.
  - Reset: factory-fresh machine.
  - Generate 24h: instant healthy history. WARNING: it ERASES
    existing readings — use BEFORE injecting faults, never after.
  - Export CSV + horizon dropdown (5/10/30 min): downloads the
    machine's history as a labelled dataset. A row is marked
    "failure" if a critical alert happened within the next N
    minutes. The horizon is the warning time you want the model
    to learn — a business decision, not a technical one.

ADMIN  (admin only)
  Add machines, edit, print QR codes (technicians scan them to
  jump straight to a machine), and two removals:
    Archive (box icon)  = hide, restorable
    Delete (trash icon) = gone forever with all data, asks for
                          confirmation
